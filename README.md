# Nature Collapse (1.20.1)

A Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge mod introducing the primeval powers of nature, the boss **Arboris**, absolute defense and elimination artifacts, and luck-altering clovers.

---

## Features

### 1. Artifacts & Weapons
* **Nature Flower (不変の花)**
  * Grants absolute invulnerability and status preservation while in inventory or Curios slot.
  * Interactive UI (Custom Hub, Effect Configuration, Naturalize Field Adjustment).
* **NaturaLize Sword (帰化の剣)**
  * Naturalizes targets into their primeval origin state, bypassing all defenses, immortality, and damage cancellation mechanics.
  * Configurable reach / raycast attack range.
* **Genesis Root (創生の種根)**
  * Used on bedrock at the deepest layer of the Overworld to awaken the primeval boss **Arboris**.
* **Miracle Flower & Miracle Root (奇跡の花・奇跡の根)**
  * Dropped upon defeating Arboris. Core crafting components for the Nature Flower and NaturaLize Sword.

### 2. Luck & Farming System
* **Clover (クローバー)**
  * Has a **0.077%** chance to drop when harvesting any mature crop.
  * Passive inventory effect: **Luck +1**, **Loot Quality +1**, **Rare Rate ×1.5**.
* **Four-Leaf Clover (四つ葉のクローバー)**
  * Crafted with 1 Clover and 4 Tall Grass in a cross pattern.
  * Passive inventory effect: **Luck +3**, **Loot Quality +3**, **Rare Rate ×3.0**, **Loot Count ×2** (doubles all generated loot drops).
  * Villager perk: Farmers employed within 16 blocks have a significantly boosted chance to offer the **Genesis Root** trade at Master level.

### 3. Boss: Arboris, Heart of the Primeval Root (原初の根源樹 アルボリス)
* Awakens from the deepest bedrock with massive seismic impact.
* Multi-phase combat featuring sacred geometry bullet hell (danmaku), laser barrages, dynamic camera shake, and a custom boss UI.
* Heart-Stop damage cap & multi-hit barrier phase (Last Stand).

## Crafting Recipes

All recipes use a cross layout on a Crafting Table:

```
    [Nether Star]
[Nether Star] [Miracle Flower] [Nether Star]  ===>  Nature Flower
    [Nether Star]

    [Nether Star]
[Nether Star]  [Miracle Root]  [Nether Star]  ===>  NaturaLize Sword
    [Nether Star]

     [Tall Grass]
[Tall Grass]    [Clover]       [Tall Grass]   ===>  Four-Leaf Clover
     [Tall Grass]
```
